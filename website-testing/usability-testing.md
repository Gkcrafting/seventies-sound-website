# Usability testing

## Purpose

I want to find out what people like about Seventies Sound, what is confusing and what I could improve before sharing it for marking.

## Who I will ask

I will ask classmates, friends, family and my teacher to try the site. I will ask people with different levels of interest in 1970s music so I can see whether the site works for beginners as well as people who already know the songs.

The feedback form is available at https://6nwnojh1fz.zite.so.

## Tasks for testers

1. Open the Home page and explain what the website is about
2. Find an artist or song on Explore
3. Use search to look for ABBA
4. Choose a genre chip and see what changes
5. Open the extra information for an artist
6. Open a larger image
7. Play a song video
8. Open the Request page and try the form
9. Use the Menu on a phone-sized screen if possible

## Fillout questions

- What did you think the website was about?
- How easy was it to find music? (Very easy / Easy / Not sure / Difficult / Very difficult)
- Which page or feature did you like most, and why?
- Was anything hard to find or understand?
- Did the search and genre buttons make sense?
- Did the website work well on your device?
- Did anything feel slow, crowded or difficult to click?
- Did the colours, text and images make the site easy to read?
- Did the captions, written video text or keyboard controls help you?
- What is one thing I should improve?
- Is there anything else you would like to say?

## How I will use the results

I will look for repeated comments rather than changing the site because of one random answer. I will record the main good points, problems and changes I make after the testing.

The form should not ask for names or private information unless someone chooses to share it. Responses will be used for this assignment only.

How requests are handled ↓

Privacy

The request button opens your email app. This site does not save what you type. Do not include private details in a request.

Credits

Music information comes from artist sites, Rock Hall and Official Charts. The photos are from Wikimedia Commons and the credits are under each picture. The logo and record artwork were made for this project. Icons are from Lucide and Feather. Songwriters and music links are under each song.

Accessibility

The intro video has captions and a text version. You can use a keyboard to search, use the form and open the extra artist information.
